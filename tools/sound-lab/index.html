How accurate is it? Pitch uses textbook physics: Mersenne’s law with stiffness inharmonicity for strings, end-corrected air columns, finite-element Euler–Bernoulli bars with a Timoshenko shear correction, and Bessel modes for membranes. Decay, body resonance, excitation spectra and drum air loading are simplified or empirical. Material values are typical, not certified. Real instruments differ through construction, coupling, player technique and the room — measure and compare before cutting wood or ordering strings.
